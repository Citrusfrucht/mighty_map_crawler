\section{The journey to a better world}

\subsection{Awakening}
The sand under your fingers feels cold, as the throbbing pain in your head dampens. \\You still hear the commands from your captain, as your ship quivers under an unknown force. This could have been a normal journey to the island of \textbf{Desperata Adventuria}, but here are we now. As your eyes slowly open, you wake up on a large beach, the surrounding sand is tinted in a coppery red, with sprinkles of an unknown substance.
